study materials

The Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE) govern the admission of facts in federal court. Focus heavily on Hearsay, Relevancy, and Character Evidence as these comprise the bulk of the MBE questions.

quick tips

  • Hearsay: Always ask: "Is it being offered for the truth of the matter asserted?"
  • Character: Generally inadmissible in civil cases unless character is an essential element.
  • Impeachment: Any party may impeach a witness, including the party calling them.
